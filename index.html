Live in Production

Master Pricing Automation Tool (MPAT)

Arista Networks — client engagement

Key stakeholders: commodity managers, sourcing leads, and category buyers at Arista Networks.

  • Studied the existing price-comparison process and found suppliers quoting in inconsistent formats and units, leaving buyers to normalise and compare costs by hand — a slow setup prone to sourcing errors.
  • Ran requirement workshops with commodity managers and sourcing leads to scope the replacement pricing engine, converting agreed business rules into a full BRD/FRD covering price normalisation, allocation logic, and cost-weighting across sourcing scenarios.
  • Wrote functional specs for automatically checking supplier quotes against historical benchmarks and flagging outliers or savings opportunities.
  • Scoped a Pricing Dashboard giving sourcing leads a live view of cost trends, price variance by supplier, and commodity-level allocation recommendations.
  • Stayed close to engineering through the build to resolve allocation-logic edge cases, then ran UAT directly with commodity managers and sourcing leads, closing defects through to go-live.
  • Continues to support the tool post-launch, tuning allocation and cost-weighting rules as buyer feedback comes in.
Result

Replaced a manual, spreadsheet-based pricing comparison with an automated engine — sourcing decisions now run about 30% faster, and the tool is in daily use by commodity managers.
